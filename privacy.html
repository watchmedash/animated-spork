Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Tips Uploader runs only on its owner's own computer.

  • What it accesses: the owner's basic TikTok profile info and permission to upload videos, both granted through TikTok Login.
  • What it stores: the TikTok access token, saved only on the owner's computer and used only to upload the owner's own videos.
  • What it doesn't do: no data is sold, shared, or sent anywhere other than TikTok. It collects no information about any other person.

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok's settings under Security > Manage app permissions. Deleting the token file removes all stored data.
